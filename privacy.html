Bottega Privacy

The published game uses Plausible Analytics to understand visits, tutorial completion, games started and completed, and requests to download the rulebook or print-and-play PDFs.

Reports include approximate location, browser and device categories, referrals, and estimated unique visitors. Download reports include paper size and whether cards are shrunk. These reports do not identify physical devices reliably.

We do not send player names, detailed game histories, or precise location. The integration does not set analytics cookies or add device fingerprinting. Plausible processes connection information to produce aggregate reports; see Plausible's data policy.

Analytics is disabled during local development. Blocking analytics does not prevent playing or downloading PDFs.
